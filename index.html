The loop

Reported ahead.
Confirmed on the spot.

A driver ahead of you taps once. Drivers near the spot confirm it or clear it. You get one quiet heads-up as you approach. Not a feed to scroll.

Confirmations extend a report's life. Disputes shorten it. Nothing stays on the map by default.
